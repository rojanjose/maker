## The gauntlet {#gauntlet}

Each product category gets a fixed test plan before we unbox a single machine.
For 3D printers that means the same four prints on every unit, the same
filament and resin batches, and the same room. *(Placeholder copy — replace
with your real methodology.)*

- **Setup:** time from box to first successful print, counted honestly, mistakes included.
- **Quality:** dimensional accuracy measured with calipers; overhangs and bridging scored against reference photos.
- **Endurance:** a forty-hour queue to surface jams, adhesion drift and firmware quirks.
- **Living with it:** noise, smell, software and what happens when a print fails at 2 a.m.

## Refresh policy {#refresh-policy}

Guides are dated, and they don't rot. When the market moves — a new machine, a
price drop, a discontinued pick — we retest and bump the **Updated** date. If
a guide's date looks stale, treat its picks with suspicion; that's the
standard we hold ourselves to.
